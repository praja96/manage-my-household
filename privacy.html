Manage My Household

Privacy Policy · Last updated September 15, 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address and display name, to create your account, identify you to other members of your household, and send you account-related emails (password resets, email verification).
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash we can never read back - never in plain text.
  • If you turn on two-factor authentication, a secret key used to check the codes from your authenticator app, and a set of one-time backup recovery codes - stored so we can verify them, not in a form we can read back as your original codes.
  • The household data you enter - tasks, categories, meal plans, recipes, and packing lists - so it can be shared with your household.
  • Your household's Pro subscription status, if you subscribe, to unlock Pro features for everyone in your household.
  • A device push token, only if you enable notifications, so we can send you due-task reminders.

What we don't collect

Location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. This app has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs - the only server it talks to is this app's own, plus Apple's own required services (Sign in with Apple, push notification delivery, and subscription verification) and Amazon SES for sending account emails.

Where it's stored

On AWS servers in the United States, encrypted at rest. Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking.

Deleting your data

Settings, Delete Account permanently removes your account and everything you created. If you own a household, you can also permanently delete it and all of its data (tasks, meals, recipes, packing lists) from Settings - this removes every other member from it too. If you're the last member of a household, its data is deleted automatically.
