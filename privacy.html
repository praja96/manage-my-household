Manage My Household

Privacy Policy · Last updated September 13, 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address and display name, to create your account and identify you to other members of your household.
  • The household data you enter - tasks, categories, meal plans, recipes, and packing lists - so it can be shared with your household.
  • A device push token, only if you enable notifications, so we can send you due-task reminders.

What we don't collect

Location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history, or advertising identifiers. This app has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs - the only server it talks to is this app's own.

Where it's stored

On AWS servers in the United States, encrypted at rest. Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking.

Deleting your data

Settings, Delete Account permanently removes your account and everything you created. If you're the last member of your household, your household's data is deleted too.
