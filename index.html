Common questions

How do I join my household's account?
Ask whoever created the household for the invite code, then use "Join Household" when you sign up.

How do I turn on due-task reminders?
Go to Settings and enable Push Notifications.

How do I delete my account?
Settings, Delete Account. This removes your account and everything you created. If you're the last member of your household, the household's data is deleted too.
